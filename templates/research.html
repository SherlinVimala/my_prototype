This is a small-scale student study (not a peer-reviewed clinical trial). Motivated by documented literature findings that rPPG accuracy degrades on darker skin tones due to reduced signal-to-noise ratio (e.g. Nowara et al., npj Digital Medicine 2025; Dasari et al.). Log more volunteers across all three skin-tone categories, with both AMCF ON and OFF, for a more reliable comparison.
